IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULES
1. ACCOUNT and PERSON are separate entities.
2. Every person has a permanent Unique Person ID.
3. A profile can exist before the real person claims it.
4. Claiming requires OTP verification of the registered mobile number.
5. Duplicate profiles can be merged with an approval workflow.
6. Relationships belong to the family graph, not to the admin account.
7. Each logged-in user sees only the family network they are authorized to see.
8. Admins manage the system; they do not automatically become family members.
9. Privacy must be built into the database from the beginning.
10. Never expose private mobile numbers publicly.
