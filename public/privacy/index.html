Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

FamilyTree ("we", "our", "the app") helps families build a family tree, connect with relatives and keep memories in one place. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. The app is available at https://family.1infinity.online and as an Android app.

Information we collect

Sign-in information. When you continue with Google, Facebook or Mobile OTP, we receive your name, email address, profile picture (if available) and, for OTP login, your mobile number. We do not receive or store your Google or Facebook password.

Content you add. Family members' names, relationships, dates, places, photos, posts, comments and other details that you or your relatives enter into the app.

Technical data. Basic device and usage information needed to keep the app working and secure.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your family tree to the relatives you allow, send invitations you choose to send, keep the service secure, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who can see your information

Your tree and memories are visible only to people with access to that tree, following the privacy setting you choose (for example "Family only" or "Private"). We do not make a private tree public.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, and Google / Meta for the sign-in options you choose. These providers process data only to deliver their service to us.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time — see Data Deletion.

Children

FamilyTree is intended for general family use. Profiles of children may be added by their parents or guardians. If you believe a child's information was added without permission, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
