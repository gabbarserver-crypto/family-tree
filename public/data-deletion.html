Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your FamilyTree account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sharmajikaoffice@gmail.com with the subject "FamilyTree data deletion request".
  2. Write from the email address (or include the mobile number) you used to sign in, so we can verify the account.
  3. We will confirm by reply and delete your account, profile and the content you created within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in details (name, email, mobile number, profile picture) and the content you added. Family members' profiles that other relatives created and still use may remain in their tree, with your account link removed.

Facebook login

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove FamilyTree from your Facebook account under Settings → Apps and Websites. This stops further sign-ins but does not delete data stored in FamilyTree; use the steps above for that.
